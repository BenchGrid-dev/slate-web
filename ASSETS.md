# Assets

- `public/benchgrid.svg`: BenchGrid mark copied from `../web/public/favicon.svg`.
- `public/slate-logo-mark.svg`: Original Slate diamond exported from the [BenchGrid Figma design](https://www.figma.com/design/3WnhzcfFAxAfcB232qdjrM/BenchGrid?node-id=1-4). Used by the header and footer; wordmark typography follows the same design.
- `public/slate-desktop.jpg`: Desktop wallpaper copied from the SlateOS project; retained as an unused demo asset.
- `public/favicon.svg`: Slate diamond placeholder, based on the system's ◆ glyph.
- `public/slate-meadow.jpg`: Original background generated with the built-in imagegen tool, converted to JPEG for efficient local delivery. No external image requests are needed.

## Image generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: ultra-wide background photograph for a premium calm open source operating system landing page. Create a serene misty alpine meadow at dawn, very pale silver-blue sky filling the upper two thirds with extremely subtle cloud texture, distant mountain ridges and a still lake almost dissolved into white mist in the center, delicate ferns, meadow grasses and tiny white wildflowers at the lower left and right foreground edges. Soft sage green foliage, blue grey hills, a hint of warm sunrise light at the horizon. The center must be spacious, airy, bright and empty for dark headline text and a UI overlay. Editorial landscape photography, natural organic fine detail, atmospheric depth, restrained colors, luminous diffused light, tranquil and sophisticated. Landscape 1536x1024. No text, no logo, no interface, no people, no buildings. Save generated output as local file for website use.

## Foundation brand artwork

Original publisher artwork is served locally from `public/brands/`. The page applies grayscale and reduced opacity to fit its quiet palette; geometry is preserved. Names identify the projects and tools used by SlateOS.

- `nixos.svg`: Official monochrome horizontal NixOS lockup from [NixOS branding](https://nixos.org/branding/) ([source SVG](https://brand.nixos.org/logos/nixos-logo-black-flat-black-regular-horizontal-recommended.svg)). Clear space and proportions are retained.
- `linux.png`: Tux from [kernel.org](https://www.kernel.org/theme/images/logos/favicon.png). Tux was created by Larry Ewing using GIMP; [usage and credit](https://www.kernel.org/category/faq.html).
- `rust.png`: Official Rust gear logo from the [Rust Foundation trademark policy](https://rustfoundation.org/policy/rust-trademark-policy/) ([source PNG](https://rustfoundation.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/cropped-rust-lang-logo-black-150x150.png)). Copyright Rust Foundation, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Displayed in grayscale at reduced opacity.
- `wayland.png`: Official logo from the [Wayland website](https://wayland.freedesktop.org/) ([source PNG](https://wayland.freedesktop.org/wayland.png)). Displayed in grayscale at reduced opacity.
- `claude.png`: Official Claude spark from the [Claude website favicon](https://claude.com/favicon.ico), converted from ICO to PNG without redrawing. Copyright Anthropic. Displayed in grayscale at reduced opacity.
- `codex.svg`: Official OpenAI mark from the [Codex developer website](https://developers.openai.com/codex/), also used by OpenAI's [official Codex extension](https://marketplace.visualstudio.com/items?itemName=openai.chatgpt).
